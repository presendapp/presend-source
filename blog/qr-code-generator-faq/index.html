Are generated QR codes permanent?

Yes. QR codes encode data directly — they don't expire and don't need an internet connection to be scanned.

Can I customize the color?

Yes. You can choose custom colors, though high contrast (dark on light) scans best.

What data types are supported?

URLs, plain text, WiFi credentials, email addresses, phone numbers, and contact cards (vCard).
