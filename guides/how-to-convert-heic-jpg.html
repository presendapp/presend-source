How to Convert HEIC to JPG on Any Device

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is Apple's default photo format. It produces smaller files than JPEG at the same quality — but many apps, websites, and older devices can't open it.

Why does my iPhone use HEIC?

Apple switched to HEIC because it's roughly twice as efficient as JPEG. Your photos take up less storage space. The downside is compatibility: Windows, many Android phones, and most web platforms don't support HEIC natively.

The privacy-friendly way to convert

Instead of uploading your photos to a random online converter, use our HEIC to JPG Converter. It runs entirely in your browser using the heic2any library. Your photos never leave your device.

Does conversion reduce quality?

The conversion preserves the original image quality. HEIC and JPEG are both lossy formats, but transcoding from HEIC to JPEG at high quality produces visually identical results.

Batch workflow tip

If you have multiple HEIC files, convert them all, then run them through the EXIF Remover to strip location data, and finally use the Image Compressor if you need smaller files for email.
