Warum die Passwortstärke überprüfen?

Data breaches expose millions of passwords every year. Weak passwords are cracked in seconds. Reused passwords let attackers access multiple accounts. Knowing your password's real strength — not just what a website's colored bar says — is the first step to better security.

Wie wird die Stärke berechnet?

The tool calculates entropy in bits based on password length and character diversity (lowercase, uppercase, numbers, symbols). It then estimates crack time assuming a high-end attacker with a trillion guesses per second. This gives you a realistic picture of how long your password would survive a targeted attack.

Ist es sicher, mein echtes Passwort einzugeben?

Yes. The analysis runs entirely in your browser. No password is sent to any server, logged, or stored. For extra caution, you can type a password with the same length and character types as your real one — the entropy and crack time will be identical.

Warum browserbasierte Hilfsprogramme verwenden?

Online utilities like password generators, text formatters, and URL cleaners are convenient, but most send your data to remote servers. Our browser-based tools process everything locally — your passwords, text, and URLs never leave your device.

This is especially important for sensitive operations like generating passwords, checking password strength, or cleaning URLs that contain tracking parameters. With Presend, you get the convenience of online tools with the security of offline software.
