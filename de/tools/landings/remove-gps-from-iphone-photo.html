Was sind EXIF-Daten und warum sollten Sie sie entfernen?

Every digital photo contains hidden metadata called EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format). This data includes your exact GPS coordinates, camera model, lens information, date and time the photo was taken, and even editing history. When you share photos on social media, send them via email, or upload them to websites, this metadata travels with the image.

Removing EXIF data before sharing is essential for your privacy. GPS coordinates can reveal your home address, workplace, gym locations, and daily routines. Camera information can be used to identify you across platforms. Our EXIF remover strips all this metadata instantly — entirely in your browser, with nothing uploaded to any server.
