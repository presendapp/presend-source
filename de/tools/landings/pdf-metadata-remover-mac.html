Was sind EXIF-Daten und warum sollten Sie sie entfernen?

Every digital photo contains hidden metadata called EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format). This data includes your exact GPS coordinates, camera model, lens information, date and time the photo was taken, and even editing history. When you share photos on social media, send them via email, or upload them to websites, this metadata travels with the image.

Removing EXIF data before sharing is essential for your privacy. GPS coordinates can reveal your home address, workplace, gym locations, and daily routines. Camera information can be used to identify you across platforms. Our EXIF remover strips all this metadata instantly — entirely in your browser, with nothing uploaded to any server.

So entfernen Sie GPS aus Fotos online

  1. Click "Open the EXIF Remover" above to launch the tool
  2. Drag and drop your JPG or PNG photo into the browser window
  3. The tool automatically detects and displays all embedded metadata
  4. Click "Remove EXIF" to strip GPS, camera info, and timestamps
  5. Download your clean photo — no trace of location data remains

Unterstützte Formate

Our tool works with all common image formats including JPG, JPEG, PNG, TIFF, and WebP. Whether you're cleaning a single vacation photo or batch-processing hundreds of images, everything happens locally on your device for maximum privacy.

Ist es sicher, EXIF-Daten zu entfernen?

Yes — removing EXIF metadata does not affect the visual quality of your photos. The image itself remains identical; only the hidden tracking information is removed. This is actually recommended by privacy experts and digital security professionals before sharing any photo online.
