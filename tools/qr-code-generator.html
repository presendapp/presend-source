What are QR codes used for?

QR codes bridge the gap between physical and digital worlds. Restaurants use them for contactless menus, retailers for product information, events for digital tickets, and marketers for campaign tracking. A QR code can encode a website URL, a WiFi network password, an email address, a phone number, an SMS message, or plain text instructions. Unlike barcodes, QR codes store data both horizontally and vertically, allowing thousands of characters in a tiny square. They work even when partially damaged — up to 30% of the code can be obscured and still scan correctly. Our QR Code Generator creates high-resolution PNG images suitable for print (business cards, flyers, posters) and digital use (websites, social media, email signatures). The codes are static — the data is embedded directly in the image, so they work forever with no scan limits, no subscriptions, and no third-party dependencies. Generated entirely in your browser — your data never leaves your device.
