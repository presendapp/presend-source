What is HEIC and why convert it to JPG?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the default photo format used by modern iPhones and iPads since iOS 11. It offers superior compression compared to JPG — meaning smaller file sizes at the same visual quality — but it comes with a major compatibility problem. Most Windows computers, Android devices, websites, email clients, and social media platforms cannot open HEIC files natively. When you try to upload an iPhone photo to a website, attach it to an email, or open it on a PC, you often get an error message or a blank image. Converting HEIC to JPG solves this instantly: JPG is the universal image standard supported by every device and platform on Earth. Our HEIC to JPG Converter decodes your iPhone photos entirely in your browser — no software installation, no file upload, no privacy risk. Your photos are processed locally and converted to universally compatible JPG files in seconds. Perfect for sharing iPhone photos with friends on Android, uploading to websites, or editing in software that does not support HEIC.
