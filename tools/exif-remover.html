Frequently asked questions

What is EXIF data?

EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format) is metadata embedded in digital photos. It can include GPS coordinates, camera model, lens information, exposure settings, and the exact date and time the photo was taken.

Can someone find my location from a photo?

Yes. If your camera or phone has GPS enabled, the exact latitude and longitude where the photo was taken is often stored in the EXIF data. Social platforms usually strip this, but email and direct file sharing do not.

Does this tool upload my photo to a server?

No. Your photo is processed entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. It is never sent to any server, cloud, or third party.

What file formats are supported?

JPG, PNG and WebP. The tool outputs a cleaned JPG (or PNG if the input was PNG) with all metadata removed.

Will removing EXIF reduce image quality?

No. The image is redrawn onto a canvas at its original resolution. Only the metadata is removed; the pixel data stays identical.

What is EXIF data and why remove it?

Every photo you take with a smartphone or digital camera embeds hidden metadata called EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format). This invisible data layer stores your exact GPS coordinates, the camera model and lens used, the date and time the photo was taken, and even editing software history. When you share a photo on social media, email it to a client, or upload it to a website, this metadata travels with the image — often without your knowledge. Removing EXIF data is essential for privacy: a single photo posted on Twitter or Instagram can reveal your home address, workplace, or daily routines to anyone who knows how to read metadata. Journalists, activists, real estate agents, and privacy-conscious individuals all use EXIF removers to sanitize images before publication. Unlike online tools that upload your photos to a remote server, our EXIF Remover processes everything locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy. Whether you need to remove GPS location from an iPhone photo, strip camera info before a professional shoot, or simply clean metadata before sharing family pictures, this tool handles it instantly — no account, no upload.
