Frequently asked questions

What metadata is removed from videos?

GPS coordinates, device model, software version, creation date, and other EXIF-like metadata embedded in MP4/MOV files.

Will this re-encode my video?

No. The tool strips the metadata container without touching the video or audio streams. Quality remains identical.

What formats are supported?

MP4 and MOV files, which cover the vast majority of smartphone videos.

Can I remove metadata from other formats?

Currently only MP4 and MOV are supported. AVI, MKV and other formats may be added in the future.

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. The file is processed entirely in your browser. It is never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

Why remove metadata from videos?

MP4 and MOV video files captured on smartphones contain extensive metadata that reveals far more than most people realize. This hidden data includes exact GPS coordinates of where the video was filmed, the device model and manufacturer (like iPhone 15 Pro or Samsung Galaxy S24), the software version, the date and time of recording, and even camera settings like aperture and ISO. For journalists reporting in sensitive regions, activists documenting events, or travelers sharing vacation videos, this metadata can be dangerous — revealing home addresses, work locations, or travel patterns. Even for businesses, video metadata can leak confidential filming locations or equipment details to competitors. Our Video Metadata Remover strips all GPS location, device information, and timestamps from MP4 and MOV files without re-encoding the video. This means no quality loss and instant processing. Everything happens locally in your browser: your videos are never uploaded to any server, making it the safest choice for privacy-conscious creators, professionals, and everyday users.
