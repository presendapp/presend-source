Frequently asked questions

What is a text diff checker?

It compares two blocks of text and highlights the differences — additions, deletions, and unchanged lines — side by side for easy review.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. The comparison happens entirely in your browser. Your sensitive documents, code, or contracts never leave your device.

Can I compare long documents?

Yes. There is no practical length limit. The tool handles essays, legal contracts, code files, and even book chapters locally.

Does it show character-level differences?

Yes. The tool highlights differences at both the line level and the word/character level for precise comparison.
