Frequently asked questions

Will compression reduce PDF quality?

No. The tool optimizes the internal structure of the PDF and recompresses images without changing their visible resolution. Text and vector graphics remain perfectly sharp.

How much smaller will my PDF be?

Typical reductions range from 30% to 70%, depending on how many images the PDF contains and their current compression level.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no practical limit. PDFs are processed in chunks locally in your browser, so even 100+ MB files work fine.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The entire compression process happens locally in your browser. Your document never leaves your device.

Why compress PDF files?

PDF documents can become surprisingly large, especially when they contain high-resolution images, embedded fonts, or scanned pages. A 20-page report with charts and photos can easily exceed 10 MB — far beyond the attachment limits of most email providers like Gmail (25 MB) or Outlook (20 MB). Large PDFs also slow down website loading times, consume excessive cloud storage, and fail to upload on forms with size restrictions. Compressing a PDF reduces its file size by optimizing internal structures, removing redundant data, and recompressing images — all without affecting readability or visual quality. Our PDF Compress tool achieves 30-70% size reduction on average, making it ideal for sending documents via email, uploading to government portals, or archiving files efficiently. The entire compression process runs locally in your browser: your contracts, resumes, academic papers, and financial documents are never uploaded to any external server, ensuring complete confidentiality.
