Frequently asked questions

How does the AI background removal work?

The tool uses a lightweight neural network (RMBG model) that runs directly in your browser. It detects the subject in your photo and separates it from the background without uploading anything to a server.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP, and BMP files are supported. The output is a PNG with a transparent background, perfect for logos, product photos, and portraits.

Is there a resolution limit?

The tool handles images up to 4096x4096 pixels. For best results, use clear photos with good contrast between the subject and background.

Can I use this for commercial projects?

Yes. The tool is free to use for both personal and commercial projects. No attribution required. The processed images are yours to use as you wish.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The AI model runs efficiently on mobile browsers. Processing may take slightly longer on older devices, but the quality remains the same.

How does it work?

This tool uses @imgly/background-removal, a machine learning model compiled to WebAssembly and accelerated with WebGL. The model (~4MB) downloads once and caches in your browser. All image processing happens locally on your GPU or CPU — no pixels are ever transmitted to our servers. This is fundamentally different from services like remove.bg which upload your entire image to the cloud. For sensitive product photos, ID documents, or personal portraits, local processing guarantees complete privacy.
