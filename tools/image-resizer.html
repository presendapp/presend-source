Why resize images for social media?

Every social media platform has strict image dimension requirements. Instagram posts must be 1080×1080 pixels for square format, while Instagram Stories require 1080×1920. Twitter headers are 1500×500, LinkedIn banners are 1584×396, and Facebook covers are 820×312. Uploading an image with the wrong dimensions results in automatic cropping — often cutting off important parts of your photo, text, or branding. Resizing images to exact pixel dimensions ensures your visuals look professional and display perfectly on every platform. For businesses, properly sized images improve engagement rates and brand perception. For influencers, correct dimensions prevent awkward crops on profile photos and story posts. Our Image Resizer lets you choose from built-in presets for all major platforms or enter custom dimensions for any use case — forms, printing, websites, or presentations. Everything processes locally in your browser: your photos are never uploaded to any server, protecting your privacy and keeping your brand visuals secure.
