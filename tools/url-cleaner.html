Frequently asked questions

What tracking parameters are removed?

The tool strips UTM parameters, Facebook click IDs (fbclid), Google click IDs (gclid), Google Analytics tracking, Amazon affiliate tags, and dozens of other tracking parameters.

Why clean URLs before sharing?

Clean URLs look more professional, protect your privacy, prevent affiliate link hijacking, and ensure recipients see the same content without personalized tracking.

Will the link still work?

Yes. Removing tracking parameters does not affect the destination page. The link functions identically — it just stops sending your data to advertisers.

Is my URL uploaded to a server?

No. The cleaning happens entirely in your browser. The URLs you paste are never sent to any external server.
