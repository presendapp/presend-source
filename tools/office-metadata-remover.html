Frequently asked questions

What metadata is hidden in Office files?

Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files can contain author name, company, computer name, total editing time, creation date, modification date, previous authors, and track changes history.

Is it safe to remove metadata?

Yes. Removing metadata does not affect the visible content of your document. It only strips hidden information that could leak privacy or confidentiality.

What file formats are supported?

The tool supports modern Office formats: .docx (Word), .xlsx (Excel), and .pptx (PowerPoint). Older .doc, .xls, and .ppt formats are not supported.

Will my document formatting be preserved?

Yes. The tool creates a clean copy of your document with identical formatting, layout, and content — only the hidden metadata is removed.

What metadata hides in Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files?

Microsoft Office documents contain extensive hidden metadata that most users are unaware of. A Word file can store the author's name, company name, computer name, total editing time, creation date, last modification date, previous author names, and even deleted text from track changes. Excel spreadsheets may reveal formula structures, hidden sheets, and named ranges. PowerPoint presentations can expose speaker notes, comment threads, and revision history. When you share these documents with clients, submit them to journals, or publish them online, this metadata is fully exposed. For businesses, leaking internal author names or company details can compromise confidentiality. For job seekers, a resume showing 47 hours of editing time or a previous employer's name in the metadata looks unprofessional. Our Office Metadata Remover strips all hidden information from Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), and PowerPoint (.pptx) files in seconds. The entire process happens locally in your browser — your documents are never uploaded, ensuring complete privacy for sensitive business and personal files.

Microsoft Office files are metadata goldmines — and most users have no idea.
