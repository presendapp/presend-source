Frequently asked questions

What is a good color contrast ratio?

A contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 is required for normal text under WCAG AA standards. Large text (18pt+) needs 3:1. For AAA compliance, normal text requires 7:1 and large text 4.5:1.

Why is color contrast important for accessibility?

Poor contrast makes text unreadable for people with low vision, color blindness, or those viewing screens in bright sunlight. Good contrast ensures your content is accessible to everyone.

What does WCAG stand for?

WCAG stands for Web Content Accessibility Guidelines. It is the international standard for web accessibility, including color contrast requirements.

Does this tool work offline?
