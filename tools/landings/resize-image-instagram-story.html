Resize Image for Instagram Story

Instagram Stories require a 9:16 aspect ratio (1080x1920 pixels) for full-screen display. Resize any photo to Story dimensions instantly — no app needed, no upload required.

How it works

Our tool crops and scales your image to the exact Instagram Story dimensions. You can choose to fill the screen (cropping edges) or fit the entire image (with background fill). All processing is done locally in your browser.

Step-by-step

  1. Open the Image Resizer by clicking below
  2. Upload your photo from your device
  3. Select 'Instagram Story' preset (1080x1920px)
  4. Choose 'Fill' or 'Fit' mode
  5. Adjust the crop position if using Fill mode
  6. Download your Story-ready image

Supported formats

Supports JPG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC input. Outputs high-quality JPG optimized for Instagram's compression algorithm.

Is it safe?

Only the dimensions change. Your image content, colors, and quality are preserved. No watermarks, no compression artifacts, no quality loss beyond the necessary resizing.

What size should an Instagram Story be?

Instagram recommends 1080x1920 pixels (9:16 aspect ratio) for full-screen Stories.

Can I resize landscape photos for Stories?

Yes. Use 'Fill' mode to crop to portrait, or 'Fit' mode to add background bars.

Will this work for Instagram Reels too?

Reels use the same 9:16 ratio (1080x1920), so yes — this preset works for both Stories and Reels.
