Why use a browser-based tool?

Your LinkedIn banner is the first thing visitors see on your profile. A poorly cropped banner looks unprofessional and damages your personal brand. The exact requirement is 1584 pixels wide by 396 pixels tall, in JPG, PNG, or GIF format. Our Image Resizer has a built-in LinkedIn preset that crops and resizes your photo to these exact dimensions. Everything processes locally in your browser — your professional headshots and brand visuals never leave your device.

How it works

1. Upload your photo — any size, any format (JPG, PNG, WebP).
2. Select the LinkedIn Banner preset — automatic resize to 1584×396.
3. Download and upload to LinkedIn — perfectly sized, no awkward cropping.
