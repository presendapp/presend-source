Why merge PDFs locally in your browser?

Most online PDF mergers upload your documents to a remote server, creating a privacy risk. Our tool combines your PDFs entirely on your device — your contracts, resumes, and personal files never leave your computer.

How it works

1. Drag and drop your PDF files in the order you want.
2. Click Merge — the tool combines them into one document.
3. Download the merged PDF instantly. No watermark, no limits.

Common uses

Combining scanned tax documents into one submission, merging a cover letter with a resume for a single application file, assembling chapters of a report from different contributors, or joining multiple invoice PDFs into one file for accounting — all common cases where several separate PDFs need to become one.

FAQ

Is there a limit to how many files I can merge?
No artificial limit — very large batches may just take longer to process since everything runs in your browser.

Can I reorder pages after merging?
Arrange files in your desired order before merging; for page-level reordering within the merged file, you'd need a dedicated PDF page editor.

Does merging affect the quality of scanned pages?
No. Merging combines the existing PDF pages as-is without re-rendering or recompressing their content.
