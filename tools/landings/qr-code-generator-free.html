FAQ

Do generated QR codes expire?
No. Static QR codes never expire. They contain the raw data you entered and work as long as the URL or text remains valid.

What can I encode in a QR code?
URLs, plain text, phone numbers (as tel: links), email addresses (as mailto: links), and WiFi network credentials.

Is there a size or length limit?
QR codes can hold up to a few thousand characters, though very long text produces a denser, harder-to-scan code. For URLs and short text, there's no practical limit.

Does this work offline once generated?
Yes. Once a QR code is created and saved, scanning it doesn't require your device or the original page to be online — only the content it links to (like a website) needs connectivity.
