How it works

HEIC is Apple's default photo format since iOS 11. While it offers better compression than JPG, it is not universally supported. Windows, many Android devices, and most web platforms require JPG. Our converter processes HEIC files entirely in your browser.

When you'll hit this problem

Uploading a photo to a website that rejects HEIC, attaching one to an email that a Windows recipient can't open, submitting an image to an online form that only accepts JPG/PNG, or opening AirDropped photos on a non-Apple device — these are the moments HEIC's lack of universal support becomes a real problem instead of a technical curiosity.

FAQ

Will converting reduce image quality?
There's a small quality trade-off inherent to converting between compressed formats, but it's generally not visible to the eye at normal viewing sizes.

Can I convert multiple files at once?
Check the tool for batch support — many users need to convert an entire camera roll export at once rather than one photo at a time.

Does this work for HEIC videos too?
This converter handles HEIC photos specifically; use the Video Metadata Remover or a dedicated video converter for video files.
