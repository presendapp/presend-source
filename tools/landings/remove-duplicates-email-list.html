Why use this?

  • Remove duplicate addresses
  • Fix formatting and spacing
  • Sort alphabetically or by domain
  • Catches case-insensitive duplicates (John@x.com and john@x.com)

Common situations

Merging contact lists from multiple sources, cleaning up a newsletter list before a campaign, combining exported CRM data, or consolidating sign-ups collected across different forms often produces the same address multiple times — sometimes with different capitalization or trailing spaces that make them look distinct to spreadsheet tools.

Why duplicate emails matter

Sending to the same person twice hurts deliverability metrics and can trigger spam complaints. Most email service providers also charge per contact, so deduplicating before upload avoids unnecessary costs.

FAQ

Does it detect "John@x.com" and "john@x.com" as duplicates?
Yes. Email addresses are compared case-insensitively, matching how most mail servers treat them.

Will it validate that addresses are real?
No, this tool only cleans formatting and removes duplicates. Use the Email Validator or Disposable Email Checker tools to check syntax, MX records and disposable domains (they don't probe the mailbox, so they can't confirm an address exists).

What format should I paste?
One email per line, or comma/semicolon-separated — the tool detects common formats automatically.
