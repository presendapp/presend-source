How it works

Most email providers limit attachments to 10-25 MB. A few high-resolution photos can exceed this limit instantly. Our image compressor lets you reduce file size with a live preview, so you can find the perfect balance between quality and size. All processing happens on your device.

Why photos get rejected or bounce

Modern phone cameras capture 12+ megapixel images that can easily be 5-10 MB each — sending just three or four as attachments can push past most providers' limits, causing the email to bounce back undelivered or silently fail depending on the recipient's mail server.

FAQ

How much can I compress before quality visibly drops?
The live preview lets you see the result before downloading — most photos hold up well even at significant compression, especially for on-screen viewing.

Does this also remove metadata like GPS location?
No, this tool focuses on file size. Use the EXIF Remover if you also want to strip location and camera metadata.
