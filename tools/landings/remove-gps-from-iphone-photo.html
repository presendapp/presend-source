Why use a browser-based tool?

iPhones automatically geotag every photo with precise GPS coordinates, the camera model (like iPhone 15 Pro), and the exact date and time. Posting these photos on Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, Instagram, or Twitter reveals your home address, workplace, or daily routines to anyone with basic technical skills. Journalists, activists, real estate agents, and privacy-conscious users regularly strip this metadata before publication. Our EXIF Remover processes your iPhone photos entirely in your browser — no upload, no server, complete privacy.

How it works

1. Upload your iPhone photo — drag and drop the HEIC or JPG file.
2. Choose output format — JPG or PNG, metadata stripped automatically.
3. Download the clean photo — no GPS, no camera info, no timestamps.
