Why use styled text on social media?

Social platforms limit formatting options. You cannot bold or italicize text in an Instagram bio, a tweet, or a LinkedIn comment. Unicode mathematical and styled characters bypass this limitation, giving your text visual hierarchy without breaking platform rules.

Which platforms support it?

Bold, italic and monospace work on Instagram, Twitter/X, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram and most modern platforms. Some decorative styles (bubble, square) may not render on older devices, but the core bold/italic/monospace set is universally supported.

How does it work?

Type your text and the tool instantly generates 10 Unicode variations. Click any card to copy it to your clipboard. Paste anywhere. The conversion happens entirely in your browser — no text is sent to any server.
