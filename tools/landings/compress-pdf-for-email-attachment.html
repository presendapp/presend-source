Why use a browser-based tool?

Email providers like Gmail (25 MB), Outlook (20 MB), and Yahoo (25 MB) have strict attachment limits. A scanned contract, a design portfolio, or a multi-page report can easily exceed these limits. Our PDF Compress tool reduces file size by 30-70% through local browser-based optimization — your document never leaves your device, making it safe for confidential business files, legal contracts, and personal records.
