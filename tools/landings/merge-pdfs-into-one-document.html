Why use a browser-based tool?

Combining multiple PDFs into one document is essential for organized workflows. Lawyers merge case files, students combine lecture notes, HR departments assemble candidate portfolios, and businesses package proposals. Most online PDF mergers upload your files to remote servers, creating serious privacy risks for sensitive contracts and confidential records. Our PDF Merger combines your documents entirely on your device — drag, drop, reorder, and merge with zero server contact.

How it works

1. Upload your PDFs — drag and drop multiple files.
2. Arrange the order — drag to reorder pages and documents.
3. Merge and download — one clean PDF, no watermark, no limits.
