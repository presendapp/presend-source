How it works

iPhone photos embed detailed metadata called EXIF. This includes your precise location, the iPhone model, iOS version, and even editing history. When you post these photos on Instagram, Twitter, or send them via email, this data travels with the image. Our tool strips every byte of metadata locally in your browser.
