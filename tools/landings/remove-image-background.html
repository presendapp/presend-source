How to Remove Image Backgrounds with AI

Product photos, portraits, and marketing images look professional with clean transparent backgrounds. Here's how to do it without uploading your images to a cloud service.

The privacy problem with background removal

Services like remove.bg, Adobe Express, and Canva offer excellent background removal, but they all require uploading your image to their servers. This creates risks:

  • Data retention — Your photos may be stored indefinitely for "service improvement"
  • Terms of service — Some platforms claim broad rights to uploaded content
  • Data breaches — Even reputable services can be compromised
  • Corporate espionage — Unreleased product photos or proprietary designs leaked
  • Personal exposure — ID documents, medical images, or intimate photos exposed

How browser-based AI works

Presend uses @imgly/background-removal, a machine learning model optimized for the browser:

  1. The ONNX model (~4MB) downloads once and caches in your browser
  2. WebGL accelerates inference on your GPU when available
  3. The model segments the image into foreground (subject) and background
  4. The result is rendered as a PNG with an alpha channel (transparency)

Zero pixels leave your device. This is fundamentally different from cloud-based alternatives.

What works best

The AI model is trained on millions of images and performs exceptionally well on:

  • Portraits — People, headshots, profile pictures
  • Products — E-commerce items on plain or simple backgrounds
  • Pets — Animals with clear outlines
  • Vehicles — Cars, bikes, and objects with defined edges

Challenging cases include:

  • Very fine hair or fur against busy backgrounds
  • Transparent or reflective objects (glass, water)
  • Extremely low-resolution images
  • Subjects that blend into the background color

Use cases

  • E-commerce — Clean product photos for Amazon, eBay, Shopify
  • Social media — Profile pictures, stickers, memes
  • Presentations — Professional slides without distracting backgrounds
  • Marketing — Consistent brand imagery across channels
  • Passport photos — White background for official documents
  • Design work — Layer subjects into composite images

Step-by-step guide

  1. Open the tool — Go to Background Remover
  2. Upload your photo — Drag and drop or select a JPG, PNG, or WebP (up to ~10MB)
  3. Wait for processing — The AI analyzes and segments your image (5–15 seconds)
  4. Compare — Side-by-side view shows original and result
  5. Download — Save as transparent PNG, ready for any use

Tips for best results

  • Use high-resolution images — the AI has more detail to work with
  • Good lighting and contrast help segmentation accuracy
  • Simple backgrounds (solid colors) produce cleaner edges than busy scenes
  • For hair, slightly overexposed backgrounds work better than underexposed
  • If the result isn't perfect, try cropping to focus on the subject
