What gets removed

Common tracking parameters like utm_source, utm_campaign, fbclid (Facebook), gclid (Google Ads), and dozens of platform-specific affiliate and analytics tags are stripped. The destination itself is never changed — only the tracking data attached to the link.

Why this matters for privacy

Tracking parameters let the sender identify exactly who clicked, when, and often link that click back to your email address or social profile. Removing them before sharing a link — especially in a group chat, forum, or newsletter — prevents that link from silently reporting back who engaged with it.

FAQ

Will the cleaned link still work the same way?
Yes. Removing tracking parameters doesn't affect the destination page — only the tracking data attached to the URL is removed.

Can I clean multiple links at once?
Yes, paste one URL per line to clean several at once, or use the batch API endpoint for up to 100 URLs per request.

Does this protect me from all tracking?
It removes URL-based tracking parameters specifically. It doesn't affect cookies, browser fingerprinting, or tracking that happens after you click the link.
