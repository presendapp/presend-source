Why use a word counter?

Essays have word limits. Tweets have character limits. Meta descriptions should stay under 160 characters. Email subject lines perform best under 50. A live word counter helps you hit these targets without guessing or manual counting.

What does it count?

Words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, and lines. It also estimates reading time based on an average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute. All metrics update instantly as you type.

Is my text private?

Completely. Your text never leaves your browser. This makes the tool safe for confidential drafts, legal documents, medical notes, or unpublished articles that you cannot risk uploading to an online service.
