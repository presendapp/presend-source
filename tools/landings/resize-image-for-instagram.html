Why resize images locally?

Online image resizers often store your photos on their servers. Our tool resizes your images entirely on your device — your portraits, screenshots, and brand visuals never leave your computer.

Built-in size presets

Instagram Post (1080×1080), Instagram Story (1080×1920), Twitter/X Header (1500×500), LinkedIn Banner (1584×396), Facebook Cover (820×312), or enter any custom width and height you need.

Why exact dimensions matter

Uploading an image with the wrong aspect ratio means the platform crops it automatically — often cutting off faces, text, or important parts of the image in ways you didn't intend. Resizing to the exact target dimensions beforehand gives you control over exactly what's visible in the final crop.

FAQ

Will resizing reduce image quality?
Reducing dimensions (making an image smaller) doesn't degrade quality; enlarging a small image beyond its original resolution can make it look soft or pixelated, since no new detail is created.

Is my photo uploaded to a server to resize it?
No. Resizing happens entirely in your browser using the Canvas API — the image never leaves your device.
