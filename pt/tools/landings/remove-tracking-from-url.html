Por que os URLs têm parâmetros de rastreamento?

Marketing platforms add parameters like utm_source, fbclid, gclid, ttclid and igshid to every link. These tell the sender who clicked, where they came from, and what they do next. When you forward that link, you spread the tracking.

É seguro removê-los?

Absolutely. Removing tracking parameters does not break the link — the page loads exactly the same. You simply stop feeding data back to advertisers and social platforms about who you shared the link with.
