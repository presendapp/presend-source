Por que usar ferramentas utilitárias baseadas em navegador?

Online utilities like password generators, text formatters, and URL cleaners are convenient, but most send your data to remote servers. Our browser-based tools process everything locally — your passwords, text, and URLs never leave your device.

This is especially important for sensitive operations like generating passwords, checking password strength, or cleaning URLs that contain tracking parameters. With Presend, you get the convenience of online tools with the security of offline software.

Como nossas ferramentas utilitárias protegem sua privacidade

  1. All processing happens in your browser using JavaScript
  2. No data is transmitted to external servers
  3. Only anonymous visit counts are recorded
  4. Your input data is cleared when you close the tab

O que você pode fazer com nossas ferramentas utilitárias?

Generate cryptographically secure passwords, check password strength against entropy analysis, convert between JSON and CSV formats, compare text differences, split long text into social media threads, clean tracking parameters from URLs, validate email lists, check color contrast for accessibility, and convert images to Base64 for embedding.
