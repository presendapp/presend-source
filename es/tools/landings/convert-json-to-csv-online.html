¿Por qué convertir JSON a CSV?

JSON is the native language of APIs and modern databases. CSV is the universal format for spreadsheets, email imports and legacy systems. Moving between the two is a daily need for anyone who works with data.

¿Están mis datos seguros?

Yes. The conversion runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your JSON or CSV never touches a server, never gets logged, and disappears the moment you close the tab. Perfect for sensitive customer or business data.

¿Qué puedo convertir?

Paste an array of objects to get a clean CSV with headers. Paste a CSV to get a formatted JSON array. The tool handles quoted fields, commas inside values, and nested structures gracefully.
